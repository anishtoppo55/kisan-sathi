# Fasal calendar — Chhattisgarh plains (कब क्या बोएँ)
Month-by-month guide to what farmers in the Chhattisgarh plains (Raipur, Durg, Bilaspur, Bemetara, Dhamtari, Mahasamund and nearby districts) sow and harvest. Week numbers ("II week") mean the second week of the month. Dates shift with rainfall and soil moisture; in Bastar and Surguja (plateau and hill districts) sowing can differ by 1–2 weeks. Choose the crop by soil (bhata, matasi, dorsa, kanhar) and irrigation, and confirm with your RAEO or KVK.

## June – July: kharif sowing (खरीफ बुवाई)
<!-- stage: sowing; topic: sowing; month: June, July -->
- Paddy (dhaan): sow from June II week to July II week (nursery or direct seeding with the monsoon). Harvest November I–III week.
- Maize (makka): sow June III week to July I week. Harvest October I–II week.
- Pulses: arhar (tur) June IV week to July II week (harvest late December–January); urad and moong June IV week to July II week (harvest September–early October).
- Oilseeds: soybean June IV week to July II week; groundnut July I–II week; til (sesamum) July II–IV week. Harvest in October.
- Millets: kodo and kutki June IV week to July I week (harvest late September–early October). Kulthi July I–II week.
- Krishak Unnati Yojana gives input assistance for pulses, oilseeds, maize, kodo, kutki and ragi, also when farmers shift from paddy (see the scheme guide).

## August – September: kharif crop care (खरीफ फसल की देखभाल)
<!-- stage: vegetative, flowering; topic: sowing; month: August, September -->
- No major sowing; the kharif crops are growing. Watch paddy for stem borer, brown planthopper (BPH) and blast, and drain excess water.
- Late September: harvest of urad, moong, kodo and kutki starts.
- Plan the rabi crop now and arrange seed of recommended varieties; get the soil tested after harvest (Soil Health Card).

## October: harvest of early kharif, start of rabi (अक्टूबर)
<!-- stage: sowing, maturity; topic: sowing, harvest; month: October -->
- Harvest: maize (October I–II week), soybean, groundnut and til (October).
- Start rabi sowing on residual moisture: linseed (alsi) and safflower (kusum) from October I week; mustard and peas from October II week; lentil (masoor) from October II week; lathyrus (teora) and kulthi from October III week.
- Chana: ICAR advises sowing between 15 October and 30 November.
- Utera (relay) crops such as teora are broadcast into standing paddy 1–2 weeks before paddy harvest, when the field is moist.

## November: paddy harvest and main rabi sowing (नवंबर)
<!-- stage: sowing, maturity, post-harvest; topic: sowing, harvest; month: November -->
- Harvest paddy (November I–III week) and sell at the cooperative society; paddy procurement starts 15 November.
- Sow: chana (gram) November II week to December II week; wheat (gehun) November III week onward — ICAR advises finishing timely-sown wheat by 25 November; rabi maize November II week onward; rabi moong and urad November II week to December I week.
- Finish mustard, lentil, linseed, safflower and teora sowing by about 15–20 November; later sowing lowers yield.
- Good month for soil testing right after paddy harvest.

## December: late rabi sowing (दिसंबर)
<!-- stage: sowing; topic: sowing; month: December -->
- Late-sown wheat: early maturing varieties between 25 November and 15 December (use a higher seed rate; see the wheat guide).
- Chana sowing should be finished by December II week.
- Rabi/summer paddy where irrigation is assured: December II week onward. Groundnut (rabi/summer) from December IV week.
- Harvest of arhar (tur) starts late December.

## January – February: rabi crop care and early harvest (जनवरी – फ़रवरी)
<!-- stage: flowering, maturity; topic: harvest; month: January, February -->
- Watch for frost (paala) on clear cold nights: light irrigation in the evening protects chana, wheat and teora.
- Harvest: lentil (late January–February), mustard (February I–III week), peas and rabi maize (February), teora (February–early March).
- Fodder maize for summer: sow by the end of February where irrigation is available.

## March – May: rabi harvest and summer (मार्च – मई)
<!-- stage: maturity, post-harvest; topic: harvest; month: March, April, May -->
- Harvest wheat and chana (March III week to April I week), linseed and safflower (February–March), rabi paddy (late March–April).
- Summer groundnut is harvested in April.
- After harvest, deep summer ploughing helps control soil pests and weeds; prepare fields and bunds for the next kharif season and get seed ready before the monsoon.
