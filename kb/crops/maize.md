# Makka / Maize (मक्का)
Maize (makka, bhutta) is grown in Chhattisgarh mainly in kharif on well drained uplands, and in rabi where irrigation is assured. It is a good alternative to paddy in upland (tikra) fields, and Krishak Unnati Yojana gives input assistance to farmers who grow maize instead of paddy. Maize needs well drained soil; it suffers in waterlogged fields.

## Sowing time (बुवाई का समय)
<!-- stage: sowing; topic: sowing -->
- Kharif maize in the Chhattisgarh plains: sow from June III week to July I week (PMFBY calendar, Raipur); harvest in October I–II week.
- Rabi maize: in neighbouring Madhya Pradesh ICAR-IIMR recommends 15 October to 15 November; the PMFBY calendar for Raipur lists November II week onward, harvest February. Rabi maize needs assured irrigation.
- Fodder maize: October–November in rabi, or by the end of February as a summer crop (ICAR Rabi Agro-Advisory, Chhattisgarh).

## Seed rate, spacing and seed treatment (बीज दर, दूरी, बीज उपचार)
<!-- stage: sowing; topic: sowing, disease, pest -->
- Grain maize: 20–22 kg seed per hectare, row spacing 60 cm and 18–20 cm between plants (about 90,000 plants per hectare); sow 4–5 cm deep (ICAR-IIMR).
- Use F1 hybrid seed of a variety recommended for your area; ask the KVK.
- Raised-bed (ridge) sowing helps in both heavy rain and limited irrigation and saves 20–30% irrigation water.
- Seed treatment for leaf blights and sheath blight: Bavistin + Captan (1:1) at 2 g per kg seed; against termite and shoot fly: imidacloprid at 4 g per kg seed (ICAR-IIMR). The NCIPM maize package also lists thiram 75% WS at 25–30 g per kg seed. Ask the KVK which product is available locally.
- Fodder maize: 50–60 kg seed per hectare (ICAR Rabi Agro-Advisory).

## Fertilizer (खाद)
<!-- stage: sowing, vegetative; topic: fertilizer -->
- Apply well rotted FYM (gobar khaad) 10–15 days before sowing; follow your Soil Health Card for the exact dose.
- For high-yield rabi hybrid maize ICAR-IIMR recommends 150–180 kg N, 70–80 kg P2O5, 70–80 kg K2O and 25 kg zinc sulphate per hectare, with 10 t FYM.
- Give all phosphorus, potash and zinc at sowing. Split nitrogen: 20% at sowing, 25% at four-leaf stage, 30% at eight-leaf stage, 20% at tasseling and 5% at grain filling.
- Fodder maize (Chhattisgarh advisory): 100 kg N, 40 kg P and 40 kg K per hectare; half N at sowing and half at knee-high stage.

## Irrigation and drainage (सिंचाई)
<!-- stage: vegetative, flowering, maturity; topic: irrigation -->
- Kharif maize: drain excess water quickly; standing water damages the crop.
- Rabi maize needs 4–6 irrigations at 20–25 day intervals. The most important irrigations are just before flowering, after flowering (tasseling–silking) and at early grain filling. Do not let the crop face water stress from flowering to grain filling.
- Skip irrigation when good rain is received or forecast.

## Fall armyworm (फॉल आर्मीवर्म / सैनिक कीट)
<!-- stage: sowing, vegetative, flowering; topic: pest -->
- Symptom: ragged holes and "window panes" in young leaves, sawdust-like droppings in the whorl (gobh). The larva has an inverted "Y" on the head and four dots in a square near the tail.
- Monitor: pheromone traps at 5 per acre; start scouting as soon as seedlings emerge. Erect bird perches.
- Act when: 5% plants damaged at seedling to early whorl stage (3–4 weeks after emergence); 10% fresh whorl damage at mid-whorl and 20% at late-whorl stage (5–7 weeks).
- Early stage: neem oil at 5 ml per litre as egg-laying deterrent one week after sowing, or 5% NSKE / azadirachtin 1500 ppm at 5 ml per litre. Put dry sand, or sand + lime (9:1), into the whorls in the first 30 days.
- Mid to late whorl stage (chemical, only after the threshold): spinetoram 11.7% SC at 0.5 ml per litre water, or chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC at 0.4 ml per litre water, or thiamethoxam 12.6% + lambda-cyhalothrin 9.5% at 0.25 ml per litre water. Spray into the whorl.
- From tasseling onward insecticide spraying is not cost effective; hand-pick larvae.
- Source: ICAR-CCARI Extension Folder 111 (2023).

## Other pests and diseases (अन्य कीट व रोग)
<!-- stage: vegetative, flowering; topic: pest, disease -->
- Stem borer, shoot fly and termites are other common pests; seed treatment and timely sowing reduce them.
- Leaf blight (Turcicum / Maydis), downy mildew and stalk rot: act at about 10% incidence (NCIPM ETL). Use resistant hybrids, crop rotation, good drainage and balanced fertilizer (avoid excess N with low K).
- Ask the KVK before spraying any fungicide.

## Harvest (कटाई)
<!-- stage: maturity, post-harvest; topic: harvest -->
- Harvest when the husk turns dry and brown and grains are hard; kharif maize is ready in October, rabi maize in February–March.
- Dry cobs and grain well in the sun before storage to prevent fungus (aflatoxin); store in clean, dry bags.
