# Krishak Unnati Yojana, Chhattisgarh (कृषक उन्नति योजना)
Chhattisgarh state government scheme that gives input assistance (aadan sahayata) to farmers on top of the central MSP. It is linked to paddy procurement (dhaan kharidi) through cooperative societies.

## Benefits for paddy farmers (dhaan kisan ko kitna milta hai)
- Paddy is bought at ₹3,100 per quintal in total. This is the central MSP plus the state's input assistance (the "difference amount" or antar raashi).
- A farmer can sell up to 21 quintals of paddy per acre.
- The MSP part is paid at the time of sale; the difference amount under Krishak Unnati Yojana is paid separately later by the state.

## Benefits for other kharif crops (fasal badalne par sahayata)
- From kharif 2026, farmers growing pulses (dalhan), oilseeds (tilhan), maize (makka), kodo, kutki, ragi or cotton, or farmers who shift from paddy to these kharif crops, get input assistance of ₹15,000 per acre.
- Aim: crop diversification and saving water.

## Eligibility (kaun paatra hai)
- The farmer must be registered on the state's Integrated Farmer Portal (Ekikrit Kisan Portal / kisan panjiyan).
- Agristack (Farmer ID) enrolment is required.
- The crop area is taken from the Digital Crop Survey (girdawari).
- For paddy: the paddy must be sold at a cooperative society (sahkari samiti / upaarjan kendra) during the procurement season.

## Dhaan kharidi 2026-27 (paddy procurement this season)
- Procurement for kharif marketing year 2026-27 starts on 15 November 2026 at about 3,100 procurement centres (upaarjan kendra), including 360 new ones.
- Farmers registered last year who have an Agristack ID do not need to register again.
- Farmers with Agristack registration do not need to show the rin pustika (loan book) separately at the society.
- Biometric verification of the farmer is done at the procurement centre.

## Token for selling paddy (token kaise milega)
- Tokens are booked on the "Tuhar Token" mobile app (Token Tuhar Haath) from the Play Store, with OTP-based registration, 24 hours a day. Token booking opens a few days before procurement starts.
- 70% of each society's daily tokens are issued through the app and 30% at the society.
- Number of tokens by land: up to 2 acres → 1 token; more than 2 and up to 10 acres → 2 tokens; more than 10 acres → 3 tokens.
- While booking, the farmer can see available dates and the society's daily purchase limit.

## Documents (zaroori dastavez)
- Aadhaar card and mobile number.
- Bank account details (passbook).
- Land records (B1 / khasra) and Agristack Farmer ID.
- Registration on the Integrated Farmer Portal.

## Where to get help
- Your primary agricultural cooperative society (PACS / sahkari samiti), the patwari, RAEO or the district agriculture office.
