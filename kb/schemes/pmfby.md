# PM Fasal Bima Yojana, PMFBY (प्रधानमंत्री फसल बीमा योजना)
Central crop insurance scheme. It pays farmers for crop loss from natural calamities, pests and diseases. Also called fasal bima, crop insurance.

## Premium (kitna premium dena hota hai)
- Kharif food and oilseed crops (e.g. paddy/dhaan): farmer pays at most 2% of the sum insured.
- Rabi food and oilseed crops (e.g. wheat/gehun, chana, teora, mustard): farmer pays at most 1.5% of the sum insured.
- Annual commercial and horticultural crops: farmer pays at most 5%.
- The rest of the premium is paid by the central and state governments.

## What is covered (kis nuksaan par paisa milta hai)
- Yield loss from drought, flood, dry spells, pests and diseases, as per the notified area crop cutting experiments.
- Prevented sowing: when bad weather stops sowing.
- Localised calamities such as hailstorm, landslide, inundation and cloudburst, assessed for the individual farm.
- Post-harvest losses from cyclone or unseasonal rain for crops left drying in the field, up to 14 days after harvest.
- For localised and post-harvest losses, inform within 72 hours through the Crop Insurance app, the Krishi Rakshak helpline 14447 (toll-free), the insurance company, the bank or the agriculture office. PMFBY WhatsApp chatbot: 7065514447.

## Eligibility (kaun le sakta hai)
- Any farmer, including sharecroppers and tenant farmers, growing a notified crop in a notified area.
- Since kharif 2020 enrolment is voluntary for all farmers, including those with crop loans (loanee farmers). A loanee farmer who does not want insurance must give a written opt-out to the bank before the cut-off date.
- Which crops and areas are notified differs by district and season. Check with the bank, CSC or agriculture office.

## Documents (zaroori dastavez)
- Aadhaar card.
- Bank passbook.
- Land records (B1 / khasra) or, for tenants, the tenancy/sharecropping agreement as accepted by the state.
- Sowing declaration (bowni pramaan patra) showing the crop sown.

## How to apply (kaise judein)
- Through your bank branch or cooperative society (for loanee farmers, usually automatic unless you opt out).
- At a CSC / lok seva kendra.
- Online at pmfby.gov.in or the Crop Insurance mobile app.
- Each season has a cut-off date for enrolment. For rabi it is usually in December; check the exact date for your district.
