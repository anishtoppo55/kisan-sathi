# Kisan Credit Card, KCC (किसान क्रेडिट कार्ड)
A revolving crop loan account from banks and cooperative societies for farming needs, at a low interest rate.

## Benefits (laabh)
- Short-term loan for seeds, fertilizer, pesticides, labour and other cultivation costs, post-harvest expenses and some household needs, plus allied activities like animal husbandry and fisheries.
- Under the Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS), crop loans are given at 7% interest. Farmers who repay on time get an extra 3% prompt repayment incentive, so the effective rate is about 4% per year.
- The loan limit eligible for this interest benefit was raised from ₹3 lakh to ₹5 lakh in the Union Budget 2025-26.
- No collateral (girvi) is needed for loans up to ₹2 lakh (RBI, from 1 January 2025).
- The card comes with a RuPay debit card for withdrawing money.

## Eligibility (kaun le sakta hai)
- Owner cultivators (apni zameen par kheti karne wale), individually or jointly.
- Tenant farmers, oral lessees and sharecroppers (bataidar / adhiya).
- Self Help Groups (SHG) and Joint Liability Groups (JLG) of farmers, including tenant farmers.
- Farmers in animal husbandry and fisheries can also get KCC.
- The KCC account must be linked with Aadhaar to get the interest subvention.

## Documents (zaroori dastavez)
- Filled KCC application form.
- Aadhaar card and a photo.
- Land records (B1 / khasra), or for tenants a tenancy document or certificate as accepted by the bank.
- Details of crops grown.

## How to apply (kaise banwayein)
- At any bank branch (SBI, gramin bank, cooperative bank) or primary cooperative society (PACS / samiti).
- The credit limit depends on the crop, the area and the scale of finance fixed by the district.
