# PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi (पीएम किसान सम्मान निधि)
Central government income support scheme for landholding farmer families. Also called PM Kisan, kisan samman nidhi, 6000 rupaye wali yojana.

## Benefits (laabh, kitna paisa milta hai)
- ₹6,000 per year per landholding farmer family.
- Paid in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 each (kist), every four months.
- Money is sent directly to the farmer's bank account through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer). The bank account must be Aadhaar-seeded.

## Eligibility (kaun paatra hai)
- All landholding farmer families whose names are in the state land records (bhu-abhilekh, khasra/B1) are eligible. The original 2-hectare limit was removed in June 2019, so land size does not matter.
- A farmer family means husband, wife and minor children.
- Tenant farmers and sharecroppers (adhiya/batai) who do not own land in their name are NOT eligible.

## Exclusions (kaun paatra nahi hai)
The following are excluded even if they own land:
- All institutional landholders.
- Present or former holders of constitutional posts; present or former ministers, MPs, MLAs, mayors and district panchayat chairpersons.
- Serving or retired officers and employees of central/state government, PSUs and government autonomous bodies (Multi Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D employees are NOT excluded).
- Retired pensioners whose monthly pension is ₹10,000 or more (except Multi Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D).
- Anyone who paid income tax in the last assessment year (income tax bharne wale).
- Registered professionals practising their profession: doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants and architects.

## Documents (zaroori dastavez)
- Aadhaar card (mandatory).
- Land records in the farmer's name (khasra / B1 / khatauni).
- Bank account linked (seeded) with Aadhaar.
- Mobile number linked with Aadhaar for OTP.
- e-KYC is mandatory to keep receiving instalments. It can be done by OTP on the PM-Kisan portal or app, by biometric at a CSC (Common Service Centre / lok seva kendra), or by face authentication on the PM-Kisan mobile app.

## How to apply (aavedan kaise karein)
- Self registration on pmkisan.gov.in under "Farmers Corner → New Farmer Registration".
- Or at the nearest CSC / lok seva kendra.
- Or through the patwari, RAEO (gram sevak) or the village agriculture office, who verify land records.
- Check status and instalments with "Know Your Status" on pmkisan.gov.in using the registration number or mobile number.

## Common problems (kist nahi aayi to kya karein)
- Instalment stopped: usually e-KYC is pending, Aadhaar is not seeded with the bank account, or land record verification is pending. Complete e-KYC and check bank Aadhaar seeding.
- Helpline: PM-Kisan helpline 155261 or 011-24300606, toll-free 1800-115-5266.
