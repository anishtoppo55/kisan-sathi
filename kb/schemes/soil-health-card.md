# Soil Health Card (मृदा स्वास्थ्य कार्ड)
Central scheme that tests your field's soil for free and gives a card with fertilizer recommendations. Also called mitti jaanch, soil test card.

## Benefits (laabh)
- Free soil test of your field.
- The card shows the status of 12 soil parameters: nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), potassium (K), sulphur (S); zinc, iron, copper, manganese, boron; pH, electrical conductivity (EC) and organic carbon (OC).
- It gives fertilizer recommendations and soil amendments (e.g. lime for acidic soil, gypsum) for your field and crop. This helps avoid excess urea/DAP and saves money.
- Cards are issued in Hindi and other major languages.

## Eligibility (kaun le sakta hai)
- All farmers are eligible. There is no land size or income condition.

## How to get it (kaise milega)
- Soil samples are usually taken twice a year, after the kharif and rabi harvest, or when there is no standing crop in the field.
- Contact your RAEO (gram sevak), the agriculture department, the nearest soil testing lab or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) for sample collection.
- Cards can be viewed or downloaded on soilhealth.dac.gov.in using your mobile number or sample details.
- Good time in Chhattisgarh: after the paddy harvest in November–December, before preparing the field for rabi.

## How to take a soil sample (namuna kaise lein)
- Take soil from 8–10 spots in the field in a V shape, 15 cm (about 6 inches) deep, mix it, and keep about half a kilo.
- Do not take samples near bunds (med), trees, compost pits or right after applying fertilizer.
- Label the bag with your name, khasra number and village.
