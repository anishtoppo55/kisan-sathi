The problem

Farm information should be spoken, not searched.

  • Scheme rules are scattered: PM-Kisan exclusions, Krishak Unnati registration, paddy procurement tokens, crop insurance deadlines.
  • Crop advice must match the crop's stage, and pesticide doses must come from trusted sources.
  • Weather forecasts don't say "should I irrigate today?"
  • Most portals are text-heavy and in English; many farmers prefer to speak in Hindi or Hinglish.
🎤 “मैं 2 एकड़ का किसान हूँ, इनकम टैक्स नहीं भरता, KCC नहीं है” → 🔊 which schemes fit, why, and what to ask next.

The solution · three saathis in one voice app
📋

Yojana Saathi

Answers questions on PM-Kisan, Krishak Unnati / dhaan kharidi, PM Fasal Bima, KCC and Soil Health Card, and checks eligibility from what the farmer says, with an editable form.

🌱

Crop Saathi

Stage-wise advice for dhaan, chana, gehun, teora and makka, plus "what to sow this month". Doses only from ICAR / NCIPM, with a KVK reminder.

🌦️

Mausam Saathi

7-day district forecast with rule-based advice: skip irrigation before rain, frost and heat alerts, disease-weather warnings.

Kisan Saathi How it works · how it is built Gnani Prisma v3.0 · Timbre v3.0 02 / 02
01 / Speak

Browser mic

Farmer taps the mic and asks; the recording (≤ 25 s) goes over HTTPS to FastAPI and ffmpeg makes 16 kHz WAV.

02 / Listen

Gnani Prisma v3.0

Speech to text with indic-auto; handles Hindi-English mixed speech, returns the detected language.

03 / Route

Keywords → Gemini

Clear questions are routed by keywords (crop, stage, district); unclear ones go to Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite as JSON.

04 / Answer

Grounded reply

Uses only retrieved knowledge base text or live data, in the selected language; returns an answer card.

05 / Speak back

Gnani Timbre v3.0

Male voice Yashraj reads the reply (Devanagari or Roman Hinglish); v2.5 Deepak as fallback.

Speed
  • Keyword fast path skips the router call on most questions
  • Fast Flash-Lite model first, backup model after a timeout
  • Voice question → spoken answer in about 4–7 s
Trust & safety
  • Answers only from retrieved text; numbers and doses copied exactly
  • Pesticide doses only from official ICAR / NCIPM guides, with source link
  • Every crop answer ends with "confirm with your KVK / RAEO"
  • Web answers are labelled and list their sources; general answers with doses or ₹ amounts are blocked
  • Keys in .env only; per-IP rate limit; 139 unit tests
